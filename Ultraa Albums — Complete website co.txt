The difference between a cheap photo book and a high quality flush mount album.

Our thick archival board is 0.5mm-1mm thick.

Prints are of 0.5 mm and final page thickness is 1mm as standard and 1.5 mm for extra thick pages, and is guaranteed to allow your album to withstand the wear and tear of everyday viewing.
